What I do

From raw NHS data files to decisions your commercial team can act on

Smaller manufacturers receive the same NHS data the big players do — but usually without anyone whose job it is to turn it into insight. That's the gap I fill.

NHS Supply Chain dashboards

Turn the monthly NHS Supply Chain files you already receive into live Power BI views of demand by trust, product and region — refreshed each month, not rebuilt.

Prescribing & market share analysis

Build market views from national prescribing data: your share, who's growing, where you're winning and losing — down to individual ICBs and products.

Account targeting

Rank trusts and ICBs by opportunity so your field team spends its time on the right doors — backed by data, not gut feel.

Reporting & data foundations

Join up your product, customer and sales data so reports update themselves — including light-touch CRM data work where it helps.

Who it's for

Built for smaller MedTech companies

I work with UK manufacturers and distributors in ostomy, continence and wound care — typically companies that:

  • Receive NHS Supply Chain or prescribing data but don't have an in-house analyst to use it
  • Make commercial decisions on spreadsheets that take days to update — or on gut feel
  • Want the analytics capability of a major manufacturer without the headcount
  • Need a clear, fixed-cost way to try it before committing to anything ongoing
How it works
