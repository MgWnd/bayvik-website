What I do

From scattered data to decisions people can act on

Most growing companies have the data they need — in exports, spreadsheets, CRMs and finance systems. What's missing is someone whose job it is to join it up and make it usable. That's the gap I fill.

Dashboards & reporting

Power BI dashboards for sales, operations, finance or market performance — live views that refresh themselves instead of monthly spreadsheet rituals.

Data foundations

Join up the sources behind the reports — CRM, ERP, finance exports, industry data — into one clean model, so every report agrees with every other.

Market & customer insight

Turn industry and customer data into views of market share, demand and opportunity — so targeting decisions are backed by data, not gut feel.

Reporting automation

Replace the copy-paste reporting cycle with one-click refresh. The hours your team spends assembling numbers go back into using them.

Who it's for

Built for companies without an in-house analyst

I typically work with small and mid-sized businesses that:

  • Run important decisions on spreadsheets that take days to update — or on gut feel
  • Have useful data sitting in systems and exports that nobody has time to use
  • Want the analytics capability of a much larger company without the headcount
  • Need a clear, fixed-cost way to try it before committing to anything ongoing

About
