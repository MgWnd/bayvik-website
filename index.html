What I do

From scattered data to decisions people can act on

Most growing companies already have the data they need, spread across exports, spreadsheets, CRMs and finance systems. What they're missing is someone to join it up and make it usable. That's what I do.

Dashboards & reporting

Power BI dashboards for sales, operations, finance or market performance. Live views that update on their own, so no one rebuilds a spreadsheet every month.

Data foundations

Join the sources behind your reports (CRM, ERP, finance exports, industry data) into one clean model, so every report agrees with the others.

Market & customer insight

Turn industry and customer data into clear views of market share, demand and opportunity, so you target the right accounts with evidence behind you.

Reporting automation

Replace the copy-paste reporting cycle with a one-click refresh, and give your team back the hours they spend assembling numbers.

Who it's for

Built for companies without an in-house analyst

I typically work with small and mid-sized businesses that:

  • Make important decisions on spreadsheets that take days to update
  • Have useful data sitting in systems and exports that nobody has time to use
  • Want the analytics capability of a much larger company without the headcount
  • Need a clear, fixed-cost way to try it before committing to anything ongoing
